REEHAA

Unifying Instance Segmentation and Risk Reasoning for Railway External Environmental Hazard Analysis in UAV Remote Sensing Imagery

Zhihong Li1 · Qing Zhu1 · Cheng Liao1,2 · Shuo Shi2 · Jiawei Zhang2 · Yuhong Jiang3

1 Southwest Jiaotong University2 China Railway First Survey and Design Institute Group Co., Ltd.3 Harbin Normal University

Overview

REEHAA is a pixel-level multimodal large language model that unifies instance segmentation and multi-condition risk reasoning segmentation for railway environmental hazard analysis. Visually grounded query contextualization and query-projected spatial–semantic unified prediction connect visual evidence with category and risk-state semantics. Progressive cross-task collaborative learning strengthens instance localization and risk-state discrimination.

Two tasks, one image

REEH-D Dataset

REEH-D contains 16,708 high-resolution UAV images and 128,231 hazard instances from six railway lines across four Chinese provinces. It covers steel-sheet roof buildings, greenhouses, dust-proof nets, and plastic mulch, with instance masks, non-mutually exclusive risk-state labels, and visually grounded descriptions. A geographically independent test split supports cross-region evaluation.
